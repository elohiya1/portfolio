Fruit Tart — Mother’s Day 2026

I used to bake a lot more often. The past few years, I’ve only gotten the baking bug once a year or so. I usually make more simple desserts, like cookies or rice krispie treats, but this May, I wanted to make something more challenging. I really like fruit tarts and thought Mother’s Day was a fitting occasion. Looking back — I may have been a little in over my head: making pastry cream from scratch is not easy. The fruit tart turned out well though! I’m excited to make one again, see if I improve, and share it with my loved ones.

Pennant — Sketch to Logo

The day that I started making this website, my sister and I were discussing activities she could do at her birthday party. On Pinterest, we came across these felt pennants and thought they were super fun. I’ve always loved crafts, and this made me realize I want to express this creativity in my personal website. I thought about how I could include a pennant on my website, and my product design class — specifically when we talked about designing logos and making sure they’re vectorized — came to mind. I decided I wanted to try and design a pennant logo or graphic I could include on my personal website. After doing some research, I found Linearity Curve to be the best tool for this. It definitely took me some time, and I learned a lot through the process (partially documented below), but I’m very happy with how my first digital design project came out. Excited to make more!
